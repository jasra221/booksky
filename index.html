Parrot Characteristics

Understand the most important traits of popular parrot species in a crisp, Instagram-like layout.

Macaws

Large, bold, social, and playful. They need space, attention, and colorful enrichment.

African Grey

Extremely intelligent, chatty, and emotional. They thrive with mental puzzles and routine.

Cockatoos

Affectionate and demanding. They love cuddles but need strong boundaries and lots of interaction.

Parakeets

Small, fast, and curious. Great for mobile-friendly homes and first-time bird enthusiasts.
